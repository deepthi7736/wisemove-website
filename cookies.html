Cookie Policy.

This Cookie Policy explains how WiseMove uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on your device. They can help websites remember information, maintain functionality, understand how visitors use a website and improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

WiseMove may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Essential website functionality.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Remembering user preferences.
  • Understanding website performance.
  • Analytics and website improvement, where enabled.

3. Types of Cookies

Type Purpose
Essential Required for basic website functionality, security or technical operation.
Preference Used to remember settings or preferences selected by visitors.
Analytics May be used to understand how visitors interact with the website and improve its performance.
Third-party May be placed by external services used by the website, such as analytics or embedded services.

4. Essential Cookies

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to operate properly. These technologies may not require consent where they are strictly necessary for a service requested by the visitor, depending on applicable law.

5. Analytics Cookies

If WiseMove uses analytics services, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to help us understand website traffic and improve our website.

Analytics technologies will only be enabled in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

Where required by applicable law, WiseMove will provide appropriate controls for managing optional cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

Some pages may contain functionality provided by third-party services. These providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Their use of cookies is governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technology or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page.
