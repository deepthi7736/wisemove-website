Overview

ZED0 at a glance.

ZED0 is a self-service car wash machine designed for convenient, customer-operated vehicle cleaning. It brings a simple, accessible car-washing experience to modern vehicle-care spaces.

Customer operated

Designed so the customer runs the wash themselves, without an attendant.

Built for vehicle-care spaces

Intended for modern car-care and vehicle service locations.

Simple to use

A straightforward, accessible washing experience.

ZED0 is hardware, not software.

ZED0 is a physical self-service car wash machine. It is not a SaaS product, a cloud platform or a business management application. Detailed technical specifications are shared directly on enquiry rather than published here.
