Platform

One ecosystem. Customers, professionals and businesses connected.

How the WiseMove product ecosystem fits together — discovery for customers, presence for businesses, and operations software for the teams doing the work.

Customers

Discover and decide

People searching for a trusted local business browse profiles, services and catalogues on Getvia, compare options, and send an enquiry directly.

Businesses

Be found and be credible

Businesses publish a verified profile with services, catalogue and contact details — building visibility and trust in one place.

Operators

Run the work

Once the enquiry lands, Vashq handles the operational half — jobs, bookings, employees, invoices, expenses and reporting.
