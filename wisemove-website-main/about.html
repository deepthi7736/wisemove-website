About WiseMove

A product company built on useful ideas.

WiseMove Consultancy is a software and product development company creating digital platforms, business solutions and vehicle-care products from Kakkanad, Kerala.

Who we are

Small team. Product mindset. Serious execution.

We combine strategy, design, engineering and technology to turn useful ideas into products people actually use. Technology is only worth something when it solves the right problem and creates measurable value.

We don't only build for clients — we build products we have to operate. Reliability, usability and long-term maintainability aren't optional when we're responsible for what happens after launch.

Why we exist

We care about the whole product.

Plenty of software gets delivered and then quietly stops being useful. We're interested in the part that comes after the handover.

01

Product-first thinking

We start with the problem, the users and the business outcome before choosing technology. That order matters — reversing it is how teams end up with something impressive that nobody needed.

02

Built for real use

Our products are designed around practical workflows and real-world requirements. Getvia and Vashq are used by actual businesses, and that feedback shapes everything else we build.

03

Long-term thinking

We build foundations that can evolve as products, businesses and customers grow, rather than optimising purely for the fastest possible first release.

The ecosystem

What we've built so far.

Three products spanning business discovery, operations software and physical vehicle-care hardware.

Business Discovery Platform

A modern business discovery and listing platform that helps customers discover, explore, compare and connect with trusted businesses — while helping businesses build a stronger digital presence.

Operations Software

Vashq is a car wash management platform built to simplify and control daily operations — customers, jobs, employees, bookings, invoices, expenses and reports, all in one system.

View Vashq
Self-Service Car Wash Machine

ZED0 is a self-service car wash machine designed for convenient, customer-operated vehicle cleaning. It brings a simple, accessible car-washing experience to modern vehicle-care spaces.
