Overview

Vashq at a glance.

Vashq is a car wash management platform built to simplify and control daily operations — customers, jobs, employees, bookings, invoices, expenses and reports, all in one system.

Jobs

Track work from booking through completion without paper or spreadsheets.

Customers

A single customer record with history, contact details and past jobs.

Employees

Assign work, track who did what and keep the roster clear.

Bookings

Manage the schedule and avoid double-booked bays and staff.

Invoices

Generate and issue invoices tied to the job that produced them.

Expenses & reports

See what the business is actually earning and spending.
